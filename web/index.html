excalidraw2vsdx

Excalidraw drawings, as native Visio files.

Drop an .excalidraw scene or an .excalidrawlib library. The .vsdx is built in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Drop a drawing here

or click to browse. Libraries become one Visio page per item.

    Rectangles, ellipses, diamonds, text, arrows, lines, freedraw, images, and groups. Roughness is dropped. Scale is 96 pixels per inch. Connectors with bindings are glued to connection points.
